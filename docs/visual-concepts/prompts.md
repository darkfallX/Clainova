# Clainova concept image prompts

Each request used this shared brief followed by its direction.

Use case: ui-mockup. Make a single beautiful high-fidelity original UI concept image for Clainova, a working payment invitation service used by African community organisers to pay a workshop speaker by phone before they have a wallet. Wide landscape artboard. Show two flat, legible, straight-on interface canvases side by side: a wider desktop organiser screen on the left and a narrower, fully visible phone recipient screen on right, clean gutter between. Intentional authentic layout, no perspective, no giant device shells, no decorative board title, no annotation outside the UI. Both branded "Clainova". Desktop shows an actionable organiser screen with one main button. Phone is the private incoming invitation, prominently legible "20 test fUSD", "Workshop speaker fee", "Collect by 30 Nov", and one button "Verify my number". Example content only. Friendly, credible, production-quality. Use custom illustration integrated into the layout. Keep the text sparse, high contrast and large; no added features or promotional straplines. Do not show fiat money, cash-out, bank transfers, coin logos, account balances, invented testimonials or financial charts. The work should look like a designer made a specific app screen in Figma, not a poster.

## 01 Postal Counter

Direction: The payment is a modern letter. Organiser review screen: a warm ivory postal counter laid out as a two-part money order. Left editable summary (masked number ending 5678, 20 test fUSD, Workshop speaker fee); right a detachable receipt with one tiny, exquisite original engraved postage illustration of a microphone inside a folded envelope. Rust CTA 'Issue invitation'. Eggshell #F6F2E7, aubergine #29212E, vermilion #AE4025. Outfit and tiny mono type. Mobile looks like the matching opened letter; spacious editorial layout, strong rules, discreet perforation and a rectangular 'Ready to collect' ink stamp.

## 02 Pass It On

Direction: A two-person handoff at a community workshop, original cut-paper illustration with visible paper grain and silhouettes of two Black workshop participants passing a pale yellow envelope. Two bold black-and-cream cutout hands form the visual bridge, quiet background. Organiser home: generous single-column form inset beneath a wide illustrated scene, title 'Pay by phone', Nigerian country prefix +234, recipient number input and 'Continue'. Mobile invitation uses a close-up of the receiving hand holding that same yellow envelope above amount. Dark teal #123E3D, golden #F4C34E, ivory #FFF6DE. Bricolage Grotesque headlines / humanist body. Intimate, cheerful, adult.

## 03 Field Notes

Direction: A speaker's honorarium recorded in the organiser's workshop notebook. Editorial studio style: ivory page, cobalt ballpoint drawings of a portable microphone and coiled cable, understated pencil rules, one pale mint tab. Desktop asymmetrical open notebook spread: narrow margin with section tabs 'Send' and 'Ledger', left aligned 'Pay a speaker' above a simple phone field, button 'Continue'; illustration lives in right margin on unruled space. On mobile show a single clean ruled note with a small drawn microphone. Cobalt #2447A5, paper #FAF7EB, graphite #24262C. Hand-inked illustrations, polished Spectral heading and Source Sans body. Real UX with no handwriting for data.

## 04 Signal Path

Direction: A payment invitation as a clear sequence of stops. Modern transit information design. Desktop main panel shows a stepper down the left and 'Review payment' centered in broad whitespace, with masked phone ending 5678 and 20 test fUSD. Right side an original abstract enamel transit route illustration with three physical stops and just one highlighted; never a map of countries. Button 'Issue invitation'. Mobile uses one vertical track with the current step 'Verify number', later stops wallet and collection below the invitation. Deep midnight #132A3A, sky #BCE3F5, orange #ED753A on light. Barlow Condensed display, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next body. Intelligible, airy, no dashboard tiles.

## 05 Studio Pass

Direction: A payment for a speaker presented as an event credential. Desktop 'Review payment' arranged around a large horizontal punch-cut ticket, with amount and masked number in the left main section, a stunning duotone screenprinted microphone and cable on the detachable stub on right; footer button 'Issue invitation'. Phone invitation itself is a vertical event pass with a cut-paper notch and strong accessible aubergine CTA. Oxblood #582542, blush #FFE4D9, deep plum #27172C. Archivo Narrow display / DM Sans body. Bold, theatrical but believable and highly legible. No barcodes, QR codes or admission controls.

## 06 One Question

Direction: Accessibility-first, a calm guided interview with very generous whitespace. Desktop heading 'Who are you paying?' on the left, one large phone input and button 'Continue'. Right a sparse, finely shaded editorial gouache still life of a folded blue note resting beside a warm ceramic mug and microphone, on pale periwinkle. Phone puts amount, note and deadline before a full width button. Dark navy #172C53, powder #E7EDFF, blue #244FB6. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next. Large type, small step indicator, strong visible labels and button contrast. Outstanding quiet typography and composition, humane public-service clarity.

## 07 Community Noticeboard

Direction: A neighbourhood arts centre's modern noticeboard. Desktop starts on the organiser ledger, showing a compact clear three-row list under 'Payments' with statuses 'Ready to collect', 'Collected', 'Refunded'; a single strong 'New payment' button. Slim top nav. A separate pinned illustrated paper flyer of a workshop presenter beside a microphone gives personality at the edge; ink-print halftone, graphic pin holes, not a collage of controls. Mobile invitation appears as one orderly pinned note. Forest #1D483E, cream #F3EFDE, tomato #BB4933. Work Sans plus Young Serif. Handsome editorial community publication.

## 08 Pocket Fold

Direction: Origami pocket envelope. Desktop a sculptural, physically accurate three-dimensional folded ultramarine envelope in a quiet pearl-white studio on left, right a sharply typeset payment review sheet with 20 test fUSD, masked phone and CTA 'Issue invitation'. A single orange fold tab catches the light. Mobile reveals a white invitation emerging from the same blue pocket, amount and controls on a flat white field. Electric ultramarine #3545BF, pearl #F5F4F0, persimmon #F68852. Geist and large tabular figures. Precise restrained art direction, dramatically rendered tactile material, no chrome effects.

## 09 Night Desk

Direction: An evening workshop, designed dark from the start. An original screenprinted night-reading-lamp illustration casts a single pool of warm ochre light onto a paper receipt at upper right. Desktop basically a dark, beautifully typeset ledger desk: invitation detail view 'Ready to collect', amount, masked recipient, deadline and secondary status 'Invitation issued', button 'View ledger'. Phone is a focused luminous cream receipt within an ink frame, dark text and distinct 'Verify my number' action. Midnight #17232D, butter #F5D890, warm ivory #FFF8E7. Instrument Serif / IBM Plex Mono sparingly / DM Sans. Elegant, warm and cinematic without low contrast or neon.

## 10 Woven Thread

Direction: The same thread joins organiser, invitation and speaker. West African contemporary textile studio influence: one custom indigo/coral woven ribbon with beautifully visible textile warp and weft travels through an open editorial spread, restrained geometric pattern, no national symbols. Desktop a neatly typeset 'Review payment' page in a broad white field between slim horizontal textile strips; two-column definition list, prominent 20 test fUSD, phone ending 5678, CTA 'Issue invitation'. Phone has a narrow woven top edge, a quiet coral stitch beside the deadline, and an ink blue primary button. Indigo #20365D, coral #D16B56, bone #F8F4EB. Contemporary Gloock heading plus Instrument Sans. Tactile and refined, never use pattern behind small text.
