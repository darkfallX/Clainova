Read from the official pages on 25 November 2026. [Rules](https://build.qorechain.io/rules) · [Prizes](https://build.qorechain.io/prizes) · [FAQ](https://build.qorechain.io/faq) · [Start](https://build.qorechain.io/start)

- **Judge:** a jury for the Post-Quantum dApp category, run by the QoreChain Association. Names are not published. Assume QoreChain engineers.
- **Judge's product:** QoreChain's post-quantum EVM lane: `pqcVerify`, `pqcKeyStatus`, scoped EVM windows, hybrid ML-DSA-87 transactions, possession-proven keys, the QCAI auditor.
- **Scoring:** works as described 25%, QoreChain tech verified in repo and on chain 25%, post-quantum correctness and security 20%, originality 15%, clarity, UX and documentation 15%.
- **Our one job:** pay someone by phone number before they have a wallet.
- **What their product visibly changes:** before, a new wallet paid directly cannot move its tokens (0 of 5). After, it collects and spends (5 of 5). On the receipt, the visitor's browser asks `pqcVerify` and `pqcKeyStatus` live: the real signature is accepted, and one changed bit, another wallet or an unregistered wallet is refused.
- **Headline number:** 5 of 5 against 0 of 5, with 11 of 11 attacks refused. Repeat it: `ORIGIN=… node scripts/live-check.mjs 5`. The direct half needs a funded `SPONSOR_MNEMONIC`. [Results](submission/live-check.json)
- **Judge path:** `/try` on the public link, about two minutes, no key, wallet, phone or account.
- **Deadline:** 2 December 2026, 23:59 UTC (3 December, 00:59 in Lagos). Read only after that.

## Known weak points

- **Phone delivery is proven once, by the builder.** On 26 November an invitation and its code reached the builder's handset as real SMS on a Nigerian network, sent from the builder's own phone through Traccar SMS Gateway, and a new wallet on that handset collected 2 test fUSD (funded `0x78366c…`, collected `0x6b720a…`). Still a team run: nobody outside the team has done it.
- **Nobody outside the team has used it.**
- **The contract checks the verifier's signature and the recipient's key status, not a signature by the recipient's own key.** The recipient's key protects the window that lets them call it. Rivals verify the user's own signature in the contract.
- **One verifier decides which wallet belongs to a number.** Its key cannot be rotated.
- **Closest rival:** Pocket Drop. Its README says whoever holds an unbound link can choose the recipient. In Clainova a link without the code is refused. Pocket Drop has far more evidence, smart accounts and a live drill.
